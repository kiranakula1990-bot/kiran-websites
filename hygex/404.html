Who We Are

At Hygex, we believe cleanliness should be smart, simple, and affordable for everyone. As part of Ennwin Industries, we specialize in manual cleaning solutions designed to make everyday cleaning easier, faster, and more effective.

From buckets and mopping systems to brooms, brushes, microfibres, and window cleaning tools, our products combine practical design, durable materials, and cost efficiency — giving you the perfect balance of performance and value.

Whether you’re cleaning a home, office, hotel, or large facility, Hygex offers the tools you can trust to get the job done right, without breaking the budget.
