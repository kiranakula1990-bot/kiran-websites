Housekeeping Supplies Checklist for Offices, Apartments & Hotels (Bangalore 2026)

A complete housekeeping kit has five groups: tools, machines, chemicals, consumables and safety gear. Getting all five from one housekeeping products supplier in Bangalore can save time, keep quality consistent and may lower costs through bulk pricing. Use this checklist to plan supplies for an office, apartment complex or hotel.

Running out of the right supplies is one of the most common reasons housekeeping standards slip. This guide gives you a full checklist, simple planning tips and the questions to ask when you choose housekeeping material suppliers in Bangalore. You can also download the checklist as a PDF to share with your team.

At a glance: the five groups

GroupWhat it coversHow often it is bought
1. Cleaning toolsMops, brooms, cloths, brushes, squeegees, trolleysReplaced as they wear out
2. MachinesVacuums, scrubbers, pressure washers, carpet extractorsBought once, then serviced
3. ChemicalsFloor, bathroom, glass, kitchen and carpet cleaners, disinfectantsRe-ordered regularly
4. ConsumablesGarbage bags, tissues, hand wash, urinal cubesRe-ordered monthly or more often
5. Safety gearGloves, masks, goggles, caution signs, non-slip footwearReplaced as needed

1. Cleaning tools

  • Wet mops, flat mops and mop buckets with wringers
  • Brooms, soft brushes and dustpans
  • Microfiber cloths (colour-coded)
  • Toilet brushes and scrub brushes
  • Window squeegees and extension poles
  • Janitor trolley with bins and caddies

Microfiber lifts grit and dust instead of spreading it, and flat mops make it easier to clean large floors quickly.

2. Machines and equipment

  • Wet and dry vacuum cleaner
  • Single-disc floor scrubber
  • Walk-behind scrubber-dryer for large floors
  • High-pressure washer for parking and outdoor areas
  • Carpet extractor, if you have carpets

Match the machine to the floor area and floor type. For carpets, see our carpet shampooing service. Confirm machine availability, demonstration, training and after-sales support with any dealer before ordering.

3. Cleaning chemicals

  • Floor cleaner (pH-neutral for stone floors)
  • Tile and bathroom cleaner or descaler
  • Disinfectant and sanitiser
  • Glass cleaner
  • Degreaser for kitchens and pantries
  • Carpet and upholstery shampoo
  • Air freshener or odour control

Use the right chemical for each surface. For product details, see our page on cleaning chemicals.

Chemical safety

Never mix cleaning chemicals, especially bleach with acids or ammonia. Keep every product in its original, labelled container. Ask for the safety data sheet for each chemical and keep it where your team can find it. Store chemicals away from food, in a cool, ventilated place.

4. Consumables

  • Garbage bags by size and colour
  • Tissue rolls, hand towels and dispensers
  • Hand wash and refills
  • Urinal cubes and toilet blocks

These run out first, so track them and re-order on a fixed cycle. Confirm product availability and pack sizes with your supplier.

  • Non-slip footwear for staff

Safety gear protects staff and visitors. Do not skip it to save cost.

6. Colour-coding: one simple rule that improves hygiene

Many facility teams use a four-colour system for cloths and mops. One common system is:

ColourUse for
RedToilets and urinals
YellowWashbasins and washroom surfaces
BlueGeneral areas such as desks and corridors
GreenKitchens and food areas

It helps stop germs moving from washrooms to workspaces. Exact colours can differ by organisation. Choose one system, label it clearly and use it consistently.

7. Quantity planning by site type

SiteMachines to considerRe-order cycle
Small office (up to 50 staff)Wet and dry vacuumMonthly consumables
Apartment complexScrubber, pressure washer, vacuumMonthly chemicals and consumables
Hotel or hospitalScrubber-dryer, carpet extractor, vacuumsWeekly or fortnightly

These are starting points. Track usage for two or three months, then set a re-order level for each item.

8. How to choose housekeeping material suppliers in Bangalore

Whether you call them housekeeping product dealers or cleaning and housekeeping supplies suppliers, look for these points:

  • One supplier for tools, machines and chemicals can mean fewer invoices and more consistent quality.
  • Ask about bulk and monthly-contract pricing.
  • Confirm delivery coverage for your part of Bangalore.
  • Request safety data sheets (MSDS) for chemicals.
  • Ask whether machine demos, training and after-sales service are available.

9. What to ask for in a quote

  • A written price list with pack sizes, not only a total.
  • The price per unit, so you can compare dealers fairly.
  • Whether the price includes delivery and GST.
  • The delivery time and minimum order.
  • Safety data sheets for each chemical.
  • Whether machines come with a demo, training and service plan.
  • Payment terms for monthly orders.

10. Common mistakes to avoid

  • Buying only on price without checking pack size and quality.
  • Using one chemical for every surface.
  • Mixing chemicals or decanting them into unlabelled bottles.
  • Running out of consumables because there is no re-order level.
  • Buying a machine that is too large or too small for the floor.
  • Skipping safety gear and caution signs.
  • Using one cloth or mop for both washrooms and workspaces.

Frequently asked questions

What housekeeping materials does an office need every month?

Mostly consumables such as garbage bags, tissues and hand wash, plus chemicals such as floor cleaner, disinfectant and bathroom cleaner. Tools and machines are replaced less often.

Is it cheaper to buy housekeeping supplies in bulk?

Often, yes. Monthly bulk orders from one dealer may reduce unit prices and delivery costs. Compare unit prices, pack sizes and delivery terms, not only the total.

Where can I buy housekeeping products in Bangalore?

EFCS supplies housekeeping tools and chemicals. WhatsApp +91 89043 12966 to request a price list and confirm availability for your requirements.

What machines does a small office need?

Many small offices manage with a wet and dry vacuum cleaner. Larger floors may need a single-disc scrubber or a walk-behind scrubber-dryer. Match the machine to the floor area and floor type.

What is a safety data sheet (MSDS) and why should I ask for it?

A safety data sheet explains how to handle and store a chemical and what to do in an emergency. Ask the supplier for a sheet for each chemical and keep it where the housekeeping team can find it.

What is colour-coding in housekeeping?

It means using different coloured cloths and mops for different areas to reduce cross-contamination. One common system uses red for toilets and urinals, yellow for washbasins and washroom surfaces, blue for general areas and green for kitchens and food areas.

How often should housekeeping supplies be re-ordered?

It depends on the site. Some small offices re-order consumables monthly, while hotels and hospitals may need weekly or fortnightly orders. Track usage for two or three months and set a re-order level.

What should I ask a housekeeping product dealer before ordering?

Ask about bulk and monthly-contract pricing, delivery coverage, safety data sheets for chemicals, and machine demo, training and after-sales service. Request a written price list with pack sizes.
