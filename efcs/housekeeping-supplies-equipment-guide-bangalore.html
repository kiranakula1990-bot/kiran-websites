4. What should you check before buying equipment?

  • Floor type and area: A machine suited to tile may not suit natural stone or carpet.
  • Hours of use: Daily heavy use requires equipment designed for that workload.
  • Operator training: Check that staff can operate and clean the machine properly.
  • Power, noise and size: Consider battery or cable, noise during office hours and storage space.
  • Spare parts and service: Ask where the machine is serviced in Bangalore and how parts are obtained.
  • Warranty: Ask what it covers and request the terms in writing.
  • Demonstration: Ask to see the machine working on a suitable floor before deciding.

5. Chemical safety basics for housekeeping teams

The wrong chemical can damage a surface or put staff at risk. Follow these basic precautions.

  • Ask for the safety data sheet (SDS or MSDS) for every chemical.
  • Check that each container has a product label, hazard information and dilution instructions.
  • Never mix chemicals. Bleach should never be mixed with acids or ammonia-based products.
  • Match the chemical to the surface. Do not use acid on marble or other natural stone.
  • Follow the dilution instructions on the label.
  • Store chemicals closed, labelled and away from food and heat.
  • Provide suitable gloves, eye protection and staff training.

7. How do you maintain cleaning equipment?

  • Empty and rinse tanks, hoses and filters after use.
  • Clean or replace pads and brushes when worn or clogged.
  • Check cables, plugs and batteries regularly.
  • Keep a simple log of machine use and servicing.
  • Arrange maintenance before equipment fails.
  • Keep the manual and warranty documents together.

8. What should you ask a housekeeping equipment dealer?

  • Can I see a demonstration on a suitable floor?
  • Who services this machine in Bangalore?
  • How long do repairs and spare parts usually take?
  • What does the warranty cover? Can I have the terms in writing?
  • Can I get the safety data sheet for each chemical?
  • What operator training is provided?
  • Can you provide an itemised quote with delivery terms?
