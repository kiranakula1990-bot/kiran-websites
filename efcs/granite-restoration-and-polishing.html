How Granite Restoration Works

  1. Inspect the granite: Identify the surface type, finish, wear, stains and areas needing attention.
  2. Prepare the work area: Protect nearby surfaces and prepare the granite for the selected treatment.
  3. Grind or hone where needed: Use suitable surface preparation only where the condition requires it.
  4. Polish the surface: Apply an appropriate polishing process to achieve the agreed finish.
  5. Clean and assess sealing: Review whether sealing is suitable and explain the recommended aftercare.

Detailed Granite Restoration Stages

Detailed Inspection

We begin with a thorough assessment of your granite surfaces to identify issues such as scratches, stains, cracks, and unevenness. This allows us to determine the most appropriate restoration techniques.

Precision Grinding

Using state-of-the-art equipment, we perform grinding to remove surface imperfections and achieve a uniform, smooth finish. This step is crucial for addressing deep scratches and leveling uneven areas.

Meticulous Honing

Honing further refines the granite surface, eliminating minor scratches and preparing it for polishing. This process enhances the stone's natural color and texture.

Protective Sealing

Finally, we apply a premium sealant to safeguard the granite from stains, spills, and moisture. Sealing enhances the stone's resistance to damage and makes routine cleaning more manageable.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How is granite maintenance different from marble maintenance?

    A: Granite is generally harder and less sensitive to household acids than marble, but both stones need suitable care. Use a stone-safe, pH-neutral cleaner, avoid abrasive methods and check whether the surface needs sealing. Marble can etch when exposed to acidic substances, so its maintenance requirements differ.

  • How often should granite be sealed?

    A: There is no single sealing interval for every granite surface. The need depends on the stone's porosity, finish, use and the sealer applied. Check the sealer manufacturer's guidance and have the surface assessed if water absorption or staining becomes a concern.

  • Can stains on granite be removed?

    A: Some granite stains may respond to a suitable targeted treatment, but the method depends on what caused the stain and how deeply it has penetrated. A professional should identify the stain before treatment. Avoid experimenting with acids or harsh chemicals, which may damage the finish or surrounding materials.

  • What affects granite polishing cost in Bangalore?

    A: The quote depends on the area, granite condition, scratches or stains, any grinding or honing required, access to the work area and whether sealing is appropriate. EFCS confirms the scope after reviewing photos or assessing the site; a universal rate is not assumed.
