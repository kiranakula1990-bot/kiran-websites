Concrete Floor Polishing Services in Bangalore

Concrete floors are strong and practical, but bare concrete quickly becomes dusty, stained and dull under daily traffic. Concrete floor polishing transforms that surface into a dense, smooth and glossy floor that is easy to clean and built to last. At Excellent Facility Connect Services (EFCS), we offer professional concrete grinding, densifying and polishing services in Bangalore for warehouses, factories, showrooms, offices, parking areas and homes.

Our Concrete Floor Polishing Services

Concrete Grinding and Levelling

Using industrial grinders with diamond tooling, we remove old coatings, adhesives, stains and surface unevenness to create a clean, level base for polishing.

Concrete Densifying and Hardening

We apply a penetrating densifier that reacts with the concrete to harden the surface, reduce dusting and improve resistance to wear and abrasion.

Polished Concrete Finishes

Choose from matte, satin or high-gloss finishes. Our step-by-step polishing with progressively finer diamonds brings out a smooth, reflective surface.

Concrete Floor Restoration

Cracks, joints, chips and pitted areas are repaired and filled before polishing, giving tired or damaged concrete floors a uniform new look.

Anti-Dust Treatment and Sealing

A protective stain guard seals the polished surface against oil, water and everyday spills, keeping the floor dust-free and easy to maintain.

Polished Concrete Maintenance

Periodic cleaning, burnishing and re-polishing keep high-traffic floors looking their best and extend the life of your polished concrete.

Our Expert Concrete Polishing Process

Site Inspection

We assess the concrete for hardness, moisture, cracks and existing coatings, then recommend the right grinding approach and finish for your space.

Surface Preparation and Repairs

Old coatings and contaminants are removed, and cracks and holes are filled so the floor is ready for a consistent finish.

Coarse Grinding

Coarse diamond tooling opens up the surface and levels high spots, with dust-controlled equipment to keep the work area clean.

Densifier Application

A liquid densifier is applied to harden and strengthen the concrete surface from within.

Honing and Polishing

The floor is honed and polished with progressively finer diamond pads until the chosen level of sheen is achieved.

Stain Guard and Final Inspection

We apply a protective guard, burnish the floor and inspect the result with you, along with simple tips to maintain the finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does a polished concrete floor last?

    A: With routine cleaning and periodic re-buffing, a properly densified and polished concrete floor can last for many years. High-traffic areas may need a light re-polish every one to two years to keep the shine.

  • Can old or damaged concrete floors be polished?

    A: Yes. Most existing concrete floors can be polished. We first repair cracks, fill holes and grind away old coatings, stains and surface damage before densifying and polishing.

  • Is polished concrete slippery?

    A: No. Polished concrete is generally no more slippery than other smooth floors when it is clean and dry. We can choose a matte or satin finish and apply anti-slip treatments for areas exposed to water or oil.

  • How long does concrete floor polishing take?

    A: It depends on the area, the condition of the concrete and the finish you choose. Most residential and small commercial jobs take one to three days, and the floor can usually be walked on soon after work is completed.

  • What is the difference between polished concrete and an epoxy coating?

    A: Polished concrete is the concrete itself, ground, hardened and polished, so there is no coating to peel. Epoxy is a resin layer applied on top of the concrete. Polished concrete needs less upkeep, while epoxy offers more colours and chemical resistance.
