Tile Polishing and Cleaning Services in Bangalore

Tile polishing restores shine to suitable tile and stone floors, such as mosaic, terrazzo, natural stone and some through-body tiles, by grinding away surface scratches and dullness and then sealing the surface. Tile cleaning only removes dirt, scale and grout stains. Glazed ceramic and glazed vitrified tiles are usually cleaned, not polished. A dull-looking tile floor does not always need polishing. In many homes, a professional deep clean is enough to remove built-up dirt, scale, residue and grout stains. The right service depends on the tile material, its surface finish and its condition. EFCS checks these details before recommending cleaning or polishing.

1. What is tile polishing, and how is it different from tile cleaning?

Tile deep cleaning removes dirt, residue, scale, soap deposits and grout stains from the tile surface and grout lines. It does not refinish the tile itself.

Tile polishing is a surface-refinishing process for suitable materials. It can involve controlled grinding or honing, polishing and sealing to improve the appearance of a worn or scratched floor. Not every tile can safely be ground or polished.

2. Which tiles can be polished?

Tile construction and surface finish matter. A tile that looks like stone may have a surface coating that should not be ground. The following is a general guide; EFCS confirms suitability before recommending polishing.

3. Our tile polishing process

  1. Assessment: Check the tile material, finish, wear and condition before choosing a method.
  2. Deep cleaning: Remove surface dirt, residue and deposits before deciding whether refinishing is needed.
  3. Grinding or honing: Where suitable, use controlled equipment to address surface wear and scratches.
  4. Polishing and buffing: Refine the suitable surface using a method appropriate to its material.
  5. Sealing: Apply a suitable sealer when required for the floor and finish.
  6. Final check: Inspect the floor and explain care and usage guidance.

If a tile is not suitable for polishing, the work may instead involve assessment, deep cleaning of the surface and grout, rinsing and a final check. Read about common grouting problems.

6. Does your floor need cleaning or polishing? A quick check

Deep cleaning may be enough when:

  • The floor looks dirty or has a surface film.
  • Grout lines have darkened from everyday use.
  • There is soap scum, scale or residue on the tiles.
  • The tile surface itself is not scratched or worn.

Polishing may be worth assessing when:

  • A suitable stone, mosaic or terrazzo surface remains dull after deep cleaning.
  • The surface has visible wear or scratches.
  • The finish has become uneven and needs professional assessment.

If you are unsure, send clear photos. EFCS can advise whether a site check is needed before quoting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between tile polishing and tile cleaning?

Tile cleaning removes dirt, soap scum, scale, wax build-up and grout stains. It does not change the tile itself. Tile polishing grinds and refinishes the surface of a suitable tile that is worn, scratched or dull, and then seals it. Cleaning comes first. Polishing is needed only if the floor is still dull after a proper deep clean.

How much does tile polishing cost in Bangalore?

The cost depends on the tile type, the floor area, the condition of the surface and whether grinding, grout work or sealing is needed. EFCS does not publish a fixed rate per square foot, because two floors of the same size can need very different work. Send photos on WhatsApp or call +91 89043 12966 and the team will give you a quote.

How long does tile polishing take, and when can I walk on the floor?

Time depends on the area, the tile type and how much grinding is needed. A small area takes less time than a full home. Ask the team for the time estimate in your quote, and follow their advice on when the floor can be used again, especially after sealing.

Can vitrified and ceramic tiles be polished?

Glazed ceramic and glazed vitrified tiles have a thin glass-like layer on top. Grinding it can permanently damage the finish, so these tiles are normally deep cleaned, not polished. Some through-body tiles may be suitable after an assessment. Mosaic, terrazzo and natural stone are the floors most often polished.

How often should tiles be polished or deep cleaned?

There is no fixed interval. It depends on foot traffic, water quality and the room. Bathrooms, kitchens and entrances get dirty faster. Many homes find a professional deep clean useful about once a year, or sooner if grout darkens or a film appears. Polish only when the surface itself is worn.

Can I polish tiles myself, or should I hire a professional?

Cleaning with a tile-safe product and a soft brush is fine for light dirt. Polishing needs grinding machines and the right method for the material, and a wrong choice can scratch or etch the floor. Never use acid on marble, limestone, travertine or terrazzo. For polishing, hire a professional who checks the tile first.
