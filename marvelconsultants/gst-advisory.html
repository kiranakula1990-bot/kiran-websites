📚 GST Audit & Compliance FAQs

What is a GST Audit? ▼

A Goods and Services Tax (GST) audit is a statutory requirement for businesses exceeding a specified turnover threshold. It involves a detailed examination of GST returns, reconciliations, invoice trails, and financial records to assess compliance accuracy and detect discrepancies.

A well-conducted audit minimizes penalties, strengthens internal controls, and keeps you legally protected.

Why Does GST Compliance Matter? ▼

GST compliance is not just about regulatory filing—it's about financial health, operational continuity, and trust with authorities.

Working with a GST compliance expert helps you:

  • Avoid late fees, penalties, and audits
  • Improve financial discipline and reporting accuracy
  • Boost your GST compliance rating
  • Build confidence with vendors, banks, and government departments

At Marvel Consultants, we help you stay ahead of compliance—not just react to notices.

What documents are required for a GST audit? ▼

Generally, we'll need:

  • GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9, etc.)
  • Sales and purchase ledgers
  • Invoices (tax and commercial)
  • E-way bills (if applicable)
  • Financial statements and bank summaries

We may request sector-specific records depending on your industry (e.g., RERA ledgers for real estate, job work registers for manufacturing, etc.).

Can Marvel Consultants assist with monthly or quarterly GST return filing? ▼

Yes. We offer comprehensive GST return filing services—monthly, quarterly, and annual—tailored to your filing schedule and business volume.

This includes:

  • Advisory on ITC claims and invoice structure
  • Support for GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C filing

What is the difference between GST compliance and GST litigation? ▼

GST compliance involves routine filing, invoice management, ITC reconciliations, etc.—all focused on avoiding non-compliance.

GST litigation starts when you face legal scrutiny—such as notices, audit objections, or penalties—and need representation, documentation, and defense under GST law.
