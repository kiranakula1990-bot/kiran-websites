## Frequently asked questions

**Q: What is the difference between RTC and Khata?**

A: RTC (Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops, or Pahani) is the revenue department's record for agricultural land, showing ownership and cultivation. Khata is the municipal (BBMP or other local body) record for a property within a town/city for tax purposes. Land that was agricultural and then converted moves from RTC-based records to Khata after conversion and inclusion in the local body.

**Q: Why does a project need a conversion order?**

A: Agricultural land must be converted to non-agricultural use by the Deputy Commissioner before it can be lawfully used for residential or commercial development. Without it, layout approval, plan sanction and A Khata are not available.
