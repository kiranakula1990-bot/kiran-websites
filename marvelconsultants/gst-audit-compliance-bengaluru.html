What is GST Audit?

A Goods and Services Tax audit is a statutory requirement for businesses that exceed a defined turnover threshold. It involves a detailed examination of financial statements, return filings, reconciliations, and related documents to assess GST compliance requirements.

When done right, it prevents disputes, reduces penalties, and provides financial clarity.

Non-compliance isn't just a regulatory issue—it affects your business reputation, finances, and operational continuity. A proactive approach to GST compliance helps you:
