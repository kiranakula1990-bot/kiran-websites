## A Khata vs B Khata BBMP

Understanding the difference between **A Khata and B Khata** is important before starting any property-related application.

### What is A Khata BBMP?

An **A Khata** generally relates to a property that meets the applicable municipal requirements and has the necessary approvals and documentation.

Depending on the property and applicable regulations, A Khata properties may have advantages such as:

- Better documentation status
- Easier property transactions
- Better access to certain financial services
- Eligibility for applicable building permissions
- Better resale prospects
However, Khata alone should not be considered a complete guarantee of legal title. Property owners should verify the complete property documentation.

### What is B Khata BBMP?

A **B Khata** generally refers to properties recorded separately for taxation purposes where there may be deviations, incomplete approvals, or other compliance-related issues.

Depending on the specific issue, B Khata properties may face:

- Difficulties with certain building approvals
- Challenges with some financial institutions
- Additional documentation requirements
- Complications during property transactions
- Need for further regularization or compliance
Each B Khata property should be assessed individually to determine the appropriate course of action.

Conversion is not automatic and depends on the property&#39;s documents, land status, approvals, building compliance, and applicable rules.

## Khata Bifurcation BBMP

**Khata bifurcation** is the process of separating an existing property record into separate records when a property is divided or ownership is separated.

Khata bifurcation may be required when:

- Property is inherited by multiple legal heirs
- Joint owners divide their property
- A larger property is legally divided
- A site is subdivided
- Ownership is separated after partition
- Separate property records are required for eligible units

### Documents for Khata Bifurcation

Depending on the circumstances, documents may include:

- Sale deed
- Partition deed
- Gift deed, where applicable
- Will or inheritance documents, where applicable
- Property tax receipts
- Existing Khata documents
- Identity proof
- Relevant property plans
- Other supporting documents
The exact requirements depend on the reason for bifurcation and the property&#39;s documentation.

## BBMP Khata Transfer

**BBMP Khata transfer** may be required when ownership of a property changes due to a sale, inheritance, gift, or other legally recognized transfer.

Before applying for Khata transfer, property owners should ensure that:
