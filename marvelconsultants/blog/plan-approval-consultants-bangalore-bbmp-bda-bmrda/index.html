Plan Approval Consultants in Bangalore: BBMP, BDA and BMRDA Guide

By Marvel Consultants | Last reviewed: 6 November 2026 | Reading time: about 12 minutes

Guides â†’ Property and Khata guides | Tag: Building approvals

Plan approval consultants in Bangalore help property owners and developers prepare building-plan applications, identify the authority with jurisdiction over a site, organise supporting documents and coordinate the approval process. Depending on the property's location, the relevant authority may be a Greater Bengaluru city corporation, BDA or another planning authority in the Bengaluru Metropolitan Region.

Planning to construct a house, add a floor or develop a property? Before work begins, you need to understand which authority can approve the proposed building plan and what that authority expects. The answer is not always the same across Bengaluru and its surrounding region. A site within the Greater Bengaluru Area, a site in a BDA planning area and a site near an outer-town planning authority can follow different approval routes.

This guide explains what consultants do, how to identify the relevant authority, what documents are commonly needed, what affects fees and how to avoid common application problems. It is an informational guide; the actual requirements for a property must be checked against its location, land records, proposed construction and current rules.

What do plan approval consultants do?

Plan approval consultants assist owners and developers with the administrative and documentation work involved in obtaining building-plan permission. They help establish the property's planning jurisdiction, review the available property papers, coordinate with the registered architect or technical professional preparing the drawings, organise the application and follow up on observations raised by the authority.

A consultant does not replace the statutory authority, architect, structural engineer or other licensed professional. The authority decides whether a proposal complies with the applicable law and regulations. Technical drawings, structural certificates and professional declarations must be prepared or signed by appropriately qualified and registered professionals wherever required.

You may benefit from professional assistance when you are unfamiliar with the online application, have several property documents to reconcile, are unsure which authority applies, or need help tracking corrections and clarifications. Straightforward applications may be managed directly by an owner with the help of their architect, provided they understand the procedure and can complete the required steps.

Building Plan Approval Consultants: when should you engage one?

Building plan approval consultants are most useful before you finalise the construction proposal or begin work. Early checks can reveal a jurisdiction mismatch, a land-use issue, an incomplete title chain, a khata or property-record discrepancy, or a drawing that does not reflect the site's actual dimensions.

  • Before buying or developing: confirm the planning jurisdiction and check whether the proposed use and development are permitted.
  • Before drawing submission: gather the property records and coordinate the plan with the registered architect or engineer.
  • When an application is returned: understand the authority's observations and coordinate the documents or drawing revisions needed.
  • For additions or alterations: check whether a revised sanction or fresh permission is required before changing the building.
  • For developer projects: coordinate planning, documentation and related government liaison requirements across the project.

Plan Approval Consultants: what is the usual process?

The exact portal, forms and sequence depend on the authority and category of building. In general, the work moves through these stages:

  1. Establish jurisdiction. Identify the survey number, village, ward or local planning area and confirm which authority is responsible for the site.
  2. Review property records. Check the title documents, khata or property record, tax details, land conversion status where applicable, and relevant layout or previous approval documents.
  3. Check planning controls. Review the applicable master plan, land use, road width, setbacks, height, floor-area limits, coverage and other development controls.
  4. Prepare technical drawings. The registered architect or other eligible professional prepares the building drawings and supporting technical information.
  5. Submit the application. The owner or authorised professional submits the forms, drawings and supporting records through the prescribed system.
  6. Respond to observations. If the authority asks for corrections, clarifications or additional records, coordinate the response and revised submission.
  7. Receive and verify the sanction. Check the issued approval, sanctioned drawings, conditions and payment records before construction begins.

Keep a copy of the submitted drawings and the final sanctioned plan at the site. Any material change should be checked with the architect and authority before it is carried out.

BBMP Plan Approval Consultants: what has changed in Bengaluru?

Many property owners still search for BBMP plan approval because that was the familiar name for municipal building permissions in Bengaluru. The governance structure has since changed. The Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) and five city corporations now form the civic framework for the Greater Bengaluru Area. The current building-permission portal is branded Nambike Nakshe and provides online services for construction permits.

For a property within the Greater Bengaluru Area, first establish which of the five city corporations covers the site and follow the current GBA building-permission process. Do not rely only on an old ward name, an old BBMP zone reference or a previous consultant's application route. The correct jurisdiction should be verified using current official maps and records.

The official portal describes a single-window clearance system and digitally signed certificates. It also provides manuals, forms and checklists. The application category, building size, proposed use and applicable rules determine which route and documents are required.

Owners looking for BBMP plan approval should therefore use the familiar search term as a starting point, but verify the present-day authority and portal before submitting an application.

BDA Plan Approval Consultants: when does BDA apply?

The Bangalore Development Authority (BDA) is a statutory planning and development authority with a defined planning jurisdiction. A property being described as â€œBangaloreâ€ or being located near the city does not automatically mean that BDA is the authority for its building plan.

For a site in a BDA planning area, the applicable master plan, land-use designation, layout status, road access and development regulations need to be checked. Depending on the site and the nature of the proposal, the relevant approval may involve BDA or another competent authority. The jurisdiction must be confirmed against the survey number and current notifications.

BDA plan approval consultants can help owners understand the applicable process, assemble the property records and coordinate the technical submission. They should not promise approval before the authority has assessed the proposal. A site with an unclear layout history, land-use question or mismatch between records and physical boundaries needs careful review before filing.

BMRDA Plan Approval Consultants: understand the regional authority structure

The Bangalore Metropolitan Region Development Authority (BMRDA) has a regional planning and coordination role across the Bengaluru Metropolitan Region. However, it is not accurate to assume that BMRDA directly sanctions every building plan across that entire region.

The region includes multiple notified Local Planning Areas and planning authorities. Depending on the location, the competent body may be BDA, BIAAPA, Anekal Planning Authority, Hoskote Planning Authority, Nelamangala Planning Authority or another notified authority or local body. The Karnataka Department of Town and Country Planning publishes approved master-plan information identifying planning authorities for different areas.

BMRDA plan approval consultants should therefore begin by identifying the specific Local Planning Area and the authority responsible for the site. BMRDA's regional role, the local planning authority's powers and the municipal body's role should not be confused. The exact route depends on the site's notified jurisdiction and the type of development proposed.

If a property is described as being â€œunder BMRDA,â€ ask for the survey number and the name of the competent local planning authority. That is more useful than relying on a broad location description alone.

Documents commonly needed for building plan approval

The exact checklist varies by authority, site, building type and application category. Commonly requested records may include:

  • Sale deed, title deed or other ownership document.
  • Latest khata, e-Khata or applicable property-register extract.
  • Latest property tax paid receipt, where applicable.
  • Survey sketch, village map, site plan and property identification details.
  • Approved layout plan or previous building sanction, where relevant.
  • Land conversion order or supporting land-use records, where applicable.
  • Architectural drawings and site measurements prepared by the registered professional.
  • Structural design or stability documents where required for the building category.
  • Owner identity, authorisation and prescribed declarations or affidavits.
  • Other NOCs or clearances required because of the site's location or proposed use.

Do not assume that every item applies to every property. Equally, do not assume that a khata extract alone proves clear title or guarantees plan sanction. The authority may require additional documents after examining the site and proposal.

What decides BBMP plan approval fees?

There is no responsible one-size-fits-all figure for BBMP plan approval fees. Government charges and statutory payments depend on the current schedule, authority, building category and proposal details. They may include application or scrutiny charges and other applicable statutory amounts. The official portal or competent authority should be used to confirm the amount for a particular application.

Factors that can affect the amount include plot area, proposed built-up area, building use, number of floors, road width, applicable development charges, location and the specific approval category. Professional fees charged by an architect, engineer or consultant are separate from government fees. Ask for these to be itemised separately.

Be cautious of anyone quoting a guaranteed approval price without first reviewing the property details and applicable fee schedule. Do not make unofficial payments or treat a consultant's professional fee as a government charge.

How long does building plan approval take?

Processing time depends on the authority, application category, completeness of the submission, technical scrutiny, site conditions and whether observations need to be addressed. A straightforward application with complete records may move differently from a proposal involving land-use questions, layout discrepancies, missing documents or drawing revisions.

Ask the consultant to explain the expected stages and any published service timelines that apply to your application category. No consultant can guarantee that an authority will approve a proposal by a particular date. The authority may request further information or decline a proposal that does not comply with applicable requirements.

Common mistakes that delay plan sanction

  • Submitting to the wrong authority because the locality name was used instead of checking the survey number.
  • Using an outdated checklist, portal or application form.
  • Submitting drawings that do not match the site dimensions or property records.
  • Ignoring land use, road width, setbacks, access or layout approval status.
  • Assuming a khata or tax receipt by itself establishes title or development rights.
  • Leaving out required declarations, NOCs or technical documents.
  • Starting construction before the required sanction is issued.
  • Making changes to the building without checking whether revised approval is necessary.

Frequently asked questions

Who are plan approval consultants and when do you need one?

Plan approval consultants help owners identify the competent authority, organise property documents, coordinate technical submissions and follow up on the application. You may need one when the jurisdiction or paperwork is unclear, the proposal is complex or you need help coordinating the process. A consultant does not guarantee approval.

Do I need a consultant, or can an architect handle plan approval?

An architect or other eligible registered professional can prepare and submit technical drawings and handle the professional responsibilities within their scope. A consultant may assist with jurisdiction checks, document coordination and follow-up. For a straightforward case, an owner may coordinate directly with the architect and authority.

Which authority approves my building plan: BBMP, BDA or BMRDA?

It depends on the property's exact location and notified planning jurisdiction. Within the Greater Bengaluru Area, the GBA and relevant city corporation framework applies. BDA handles matters within its notified planning area. In the wider metropolitan region, another Local Planning Authority may be responsible. Verify the survey number and competent authority before applying.

Are plan approval agents the same as consultants?

The terms are often used informally for people who assist with applications and follow-up. Check the person's actual scope, experience, fee breakup and professional credentials. Technical drawings and certificates must be handled by appropriately qualified and registered professionals where required.

What documents are needed for building plan approval in Bangalore?

Common records include ownership documents, khata or applicable property records, tax receipts, survey and site details, layout or previous approval documents, land-use or conversion records where applicable, technical drawings and prescribed declarations. The exact checklist depends on the authority, site and building category.

What are the BBMP plan approval fees?

There is no single fee applicable to every property. Government charges depend on the current schedule, application category, plot and built-up area, building use and other applicable factors. Confirm the payable amount through the current official portal or authority. Professional fees are separate from statutory charges.

How long does plan approval take?

Processing time varies with the authority, application type, completeness of documents, technical scrutiny and whether corrections or additional records are requested. Check any published service timeline for your category. No consultant can guarantee an approval date.

Can the same consultant handle BBMP, BDA and BMRDA approvals?

A consultant may assist across multiple jurisdictions, but the competent authority and procedure still depend on the property location. Marvel Consultants assists with BBMP/GBA, BDA and BMRDA-region plan-approval coordination. The specific local planning authority and scope should be confirmed for each site.

Is an A-Khata or e-Khata needed for plan approval?

Property-record requirements depend on the authority and application category. A khata or e-Khata entry should not be treated as proof of clear title or as a guarantee of plan sanction. Check the current checklist for the exact site and resolve any mismatch in property records before applying.

What happens if I build without plan approval?

Construction without the required sanction can lead to enforcement notices, penalties or other action under applicable laws. It can also create problems with utilities, loans, sale or later approvals. Obtain the required permission before starting work and build in accordance with the sanctioned plan.
