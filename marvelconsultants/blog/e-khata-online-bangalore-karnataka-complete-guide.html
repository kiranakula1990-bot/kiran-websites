Complete Guide to E-Khata Online in Bangalore & Karnataka – Digital Property Records Explained

In the world of real estate, maintaining accurate property records and documentation is extremely important. In Karnataka, property management has become more transparent and convenient with the introduction of E Khata. It is the state's digital land record system that has replaced traditional paper-based property records. With BBMP E Khata online, property owners no longer need to depend on lengthy paperwork and manual processes.

This initiative is part of the Karnataka government's push toward digitalisation. Through E Khata online portals, property owners can easily manage and access their property records in a secure digital format.

E Khata provides a centralised online platform for property registration, tax payment, and ownership verification. This digital property record helps prevent fraud, speeds up transactions, and ensures easy access to property information for homeowners, buyers, and legal professionals.

Whether you want to apply for property documents, pay property tax, or verify ownership records, E Khata brings everything online and simplifies the entire process with secure and real-time updates.

Why This Guide Matters

E Khata is now mandatory for online building plan approvals in Karnataka from July 1, 2025. This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know about obtaining your E Khata certificate online.

Understanding E-Khata and Its Importance

BBMP E Khata is an electronic legal property document issued by the local municipal authority. E Khata is the digital version of the traditional Khata certificate and is managed by local bodies such as BBMP (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike) in Bengaluru.

What Exactly is E-Khata?

E Khata, often searched online as what is e khata or e khata online, is the digital format of the traditional Khata document issued for properties in Karnataka.

It contains complete digital records such as:

  • Property ownership details
  • Property tax payment history
  • Transaction records
  • Property identification information

E Khata serves as legal proof of property ownership and confirms that the property has received the necessary municipal approvals. Unlike physical records that are prone to errors or manipulation, E Khata is more secure, reliable, and tamper-proof.

Important Update

From July 1, 2025, submitting an E Khata is mandatory for online building plan approvals, including applications under the Nambike Nakshe scheme. This has made E Khata a key part of Karnataka's urban governance system.

E Khata ensures:

  • Centralised digital records accessible anytime, anywhere
  • Secure and tamper-proof documentation
  • Real-time ownership updates
  • Easy online property tax payments

E Khata is more than just a digital document — it plays a major role in modern property management. Here's why it is important:

Step-by-Step E-Khata Application Process

Karnataka's E Khata system simplifies property documentation through digital records and online application procedures. Below is a complete guide to applying for E Khata online, including eligibility and required documents.

Who is Eligible to Apply for E-Khata?

To apply for E Khata online, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • The property must fall under BBMP jurisdiction
  • Applicable for residential, commercial, and industrial properties

Eligible applicants include:

  • Property owners
  • Joint owners
  • Legal heirs
  • Builders and developers
  • Companies and LLPs

Applicants must be 18 years or older with a valid bank account.

Not Eligible for E-Khata

  • Properties involved in legal disputes
  • Unauthorized constructions
  • Agricultural land (handled under RTC records)

Required Documents for E-Khata Application

Before applying for E Khata online in Bengaluru, make sure you have the following documents.

For BDA Allotted Sites / Flats

  • Allotment Letter
  • Lease-cum-Sale Deed / Sale Deed
  • Possession Certificate
  • Encumbrance Certificate (Form-15)
  • Identity Proof
  • Bank NOC (if the property has a loan)

For BDA Approved Private Layout / Apartment

  • Layout or Apartment Plan Approval
  • Release Order
  • Possession Certificate (if available)
  • Allotment Letter (if available)
  • Sale Deed / Lease-cum-Sale Deed
  • Encumbrance Certificate
  • ID Proof
  • Society NOC (if applicable)
  • Bank NOC (if property is under loan)

How to Apply for E-Khata Online

Follow these steps to apply for an E Khata certificate:

  1. Visit the Seva Sindhu Karnataka Portal

    Go to the official Seva Sindhu Karnataka portal and click on Suvidha Services.

  2. Select BDA (Bangalore Development Authority)

    Choose Application for New E Khata. Read eligibility and fee details carefully.

  3. Register Using Your Aadhaar Number

    Complete the Aadhaar-based registration process to proceed with the application.

  4. Enter Property Details

    Fill in the required property details such as Property ID, Owner name, and Property size.

  5. Upload Required Documents

    Upload all the necessary documents as listed in the requirements section.

  6. Complete E-Sign Verification

    Verify your identity through the e-sign process.

  7. Review and Submit

    Review all the details carefully and submit the application. You will receive an acknowledgement number. Verification usually takes up to 15 days.

Pro Tip

Once approved, you can download your digitally signed E Khata certificate from the portal.

How to Check Your E-Khata Status Online

If you have applied for E Khata, you can easily track your application.

Steps to Check Status

  1. Visit the Seva Sindhu portal.
  2. Select your preferred language.
  3. Click Track Application Status.
  4. Enter your application number.
  5. View your application status.

Possible Application Status

Status Meaning
Approved Your E Khata is ready for download.
Pending Application is under review.
Rejected There may be document errors or incorrect details.

How to Download Your E-Khata Certificate

Downloading your E Khata certificate is the final step of the registration process.

Why You Need the E-Khata Certificate

It is required for:

  • Buying or selling property
  • Applying for home loans
  • Property tax payments
  • Legal dispute resolution
  • Utility connections like water or electricity

Steps to Download E-Khata

  1. Log in to the Seva Sindhu Portal

    Access the portal using your registered credentials.

  2. Check Application Status

    Verify that your application has been approved.

  3. Go to Issue Document

    Once approved, navigate to the Issue Document section.

  4. Click Output Certificate

    Select the Output Certificate option to generate your E Khata.

  5. Download the E Khata PDF

    Download and save the PDF. Always verify the details such as property information, owner name, digital seal and signature.

Common Issues During E-Khata Application

Although the process is online, some users may face issues such as:

Missing Documents

Incomplete document submission during the application process.

Website Technical Errors

Slow portal response or technical errors on the website.

Delay in Status Updates

Application status may take time to reflect changes.

Incorrect Property Details

Errors in property information entered during application.

Pending Property Tax Dues

Outstanding tax payments can block the application.

Tips to Avoid Delays

  • Double-check all documents before uploading
  • Apply during non-peak hours
  • Clear pending taxes
  • Ensure correct property details

If problems continue, you can contact the BBMP office or raise a support request on the portal.

SEO FAQ – BBMP E-Khata Services in Bangalore

1. What are BBMP E-Khata services in Bangalore?

BBMP E Khata services help property owners convert or obtain their Khata certificate in digital format. This process allows easy property record verification, tax payment, and smoother property transactions in Bangalore.

2. Why is E-Khata important for property owners in Bangalore?

E Khata is important because it ensures your property details are officially recorded with BBMP. It is often required for property registration, building plan approval, and applying for loans.

3. What is the difference between A-Khata, B-Khata, and E-Khata?

A-Khata is issued for legally approved properties, B-Khata is given to properties that may not fully comply with regulations, and E Khata is the digital version of the Khata record available online.

4. How can I apply for E-Khata registration in Bangalore?

You can apply for E Khata through the BBMP online portal or with the help of professional E Khata consultants who handle the documentation and submission process.

5. How long does the E-Khata registration process take?

The time required depends on document verification and BBMP approval. If all documents are correct, the process usually takes a few days to a few weeks.

6. What documents are required for E-Khata application?

Common documents include the sale deed, property tax receipts, owner's identity proof, property details, and previous Khata records.

7. Can I convert B-Khata to E-Khata?

Yes, in many cases B-Khata properties can be converted to E Khata after meeting BBMP requirements and completing the necessary documentation.

8. Is there a government fee for E-Khata registration?

Yes, BBMP charges a processing fee depending on the property type and application details.

9. How can I verify my BBMP E-Khata online?

You can verify your E Khata using the verification number or QR code available on the certificate through the official BBMP portal.

10. Why should I choose professional E-Khata consultants in Bangalore?

Professional consultants help speed up the process, ensure proper documentation, and reduce the chances of application rejection or delays.
