## Understanding E-Khata and Its Importance

BBMP E Khata is an electronic legal property document issued by the local municipal authority. E Khata is the digital version of the traditional Khata certificate and is managed by local bodies such as BBMP (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike) in Bengaluru.

### What Exactly is E-Khata?

E Khata, often searched online as what is e khata or e khata online, is the digital format of the traditional Khata document issued for properties in Karnataka.

It contains complete digital records such as:

- Property ownership details
- Property tax payment history
- Transaction records
- Property identification information
E Khata serves as legal proof of property ownership and confirms that the property has received the necessary municipal approvals. Unlike physical records that are prone to errors or manipulation, E Khata is more secure, reliable, and tamper-proof.

#### Important Update

From July 1, 2025, submitting an E Khata is mandatory for online building plan approvals, including applications under the Nambike Nakshe scheme. This has made E Khata a key part of Karnataka's urban governance system.

E Khata ensures:

- Centralised digital records accessible anytime, anywhere
- Secure and tamper-proof documentation
- Real-time ownership updates
- Easy online property tax payments

## Step-by-Step E-Khata Application Process

Karnataka's E Khata system simplifies property documentation through digital records and online application procedures. Below is a complete guide to applying for E Khata online, including eligibility and required documents.

### Who is Eligible to Apply for E-Khata?

To apply for E Khata online, applicants must meet the following criteria:

- The property must fall under BBMP jurisdiction
- Applicable for residential, commercial, and industrial properties
Eligible applicants include:

- Property owners
- Joint owners
- Legal heirs
- Builders and developers
- Companies and LLPs
Applicants must be 18 years or older with a valid bank account.

#### Not Eligible for E-Khata

- Properties involved in legal disputes
- Unauthorized constructions
- Agricultural land (handled under RTC records)

## Required Documents for E-Khata Application

Before applying for E Khata online in Bengaluru, make sure you have the following documents.

### For BDA Allotted Sites / Flats

- Allotment Letter
- Lease-cum-Sale Deed / Sale Deed
- Possession Certificate
- Encumbrance Certificate (Form-15)
- Identity Proof
- Bank NOC (if the property has a loan)

### For BDA Approved Private Layout / Apartment

- Layout or Apartment Plan Approval
- Release Order
- Possession Certificate (if available)
- Allotment Letter (if available)
- Sale Deed / Lease-cum-Sale Deed
- Encumbrance Certificate
- ID Proof
- Society NOC (if applicable)
- Bank NOC (if property is under loan)

## How to Apply for E-Khata Online

Follow these steps to apply for an E Khata certificate:

- Visit the Seva Sindhu Karnataka Portal Go to the official Seva Sindhu Karnataka portal and click on Suvidha Services.
- Select BDA (Bangalore Development Authority) Choose Application for New E Khata. Read eligibility and fee details carefully.
- Register Using Your Aadhaar Number Complete the Aadhaar-based registration process to proceed with the application.
- Enter Property Details Fill in the required property details such as Property ID, Owner name, and Property size.
- Upload Required Documents Upload all the necessary documents as listed in the requirements section.
- Complete E-Sign Verification Verify your identity through the e-sign process.
- Review and Submit Review all the details carefully and submit the application. You will receive an acknowledgement number. Verification usually takes up to 15 days.

#### Pro Tip

Once approved, you can download your digitally signed E Khata certificate from the portal.

## How to Download Your E-Khata Certificate

Downloading your E Khata certificate is the final step of the registration process.

### Why You Need the E-Khata Certificate

It is required for:

- Buying or selling property
- Applying for home loans
- Property tax payments
- Legal dispute resolution
- Utility connections like water or electricity

### Steps to Download E-Khata

- Log in to the Seva Sindhu Portal Access the portal using your registered credentials.
- Check Application Status Verify that your application has been approved.
- Go to Issue Document Once approved, navigate to the Issue Document section.
- Click Output Certificate Select the Output Certificate option to generate your E Khata.
- Download the E Khata PDF Download and save the PDF. Always verify the details such as property information, owner name, digital seal and signature.
