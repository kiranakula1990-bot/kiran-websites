How to Convert B-Khata to A-Khata in Bangalore

Converting B-Khata to A-Khata in Bangalore means applying through the BBMP-GBA e-Aasthi portal with the required property documents, completing Aadhaar e-KYC, confirming the property location, paying the applicable fees, and completing BBMP verification and inspection.

This page gives you the process step by step. For eligibility, detailed document explanations and the broader conversion guide, see our complete B-Khata to A-Khata conversion guide.

Documents to Prepare Before You Apply

  • Registered sale/title deed.
  • Latest property tax receipts showing nil dues.
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC).
  • Aadhaar cards of all owners.
  • Existing B-Khata/E-Khata with EPID.
  • Latest BESCOM bill.
  • Site plan/building sketch.
  • Recent property photographs.

See the complete guide for who can apply and what each document is used for.

How to Convert B-Khata to A-Khata Online

  1. Log in to the BBMP-GBA e-Aasthi portal.

    Use your mobile number and OTP to access the e-Aasthi application system.

  2. Raise a new request.

    Select the B-Khata to A-Khata conversion option and enter the property's E-Khata EPID.

  3. Complete Aadhaar e-KYC.

    Complete Aadhaar e-KYC for every listed owner. The system generates an Application ID. Save this ID for future reference.

  4. Confirm the property location.

    Pin the exact location of the property on the map because the inspection team will use the submitted location details.

  5. Declare the revenue survey status.

    Provide the required revenue survey information and upload the DC conversion order if the property was previously agricultural land.

  6. Upload the required documents.

    Upload the complete document set and self-certify that the information and documents submitted are correct.

  7. Pay the application fee.

    Pay the ₹500 application fee and download or save the acknowledgement.

  8. Complete the ground inspection.

    BBMP's inspection team verifies the property and submitted details through physical inspection where required.

  9. Pay the conversion fee and receive A-Khata.

    After approval, pay the applicable conversion charge. The digital A-Khata is then issued through the system.

How to Apply Offline

  1. Confirm eligibility. Make sure the property has a valid B-Khata, cleared property taxes, road access and no major unresolved violations.
  2. Collect the application form. Visit the jurisdictional ARO or BBMP zonal office.
  3. Submit the documents. Provide the completed form, supporting documents and applicable fees.
  4. Complete verification. BBMP carries out document scrutiny and physical verification as required.
  5. Receive the A-Khata. After approval, the A-Khata is issued. Offline applications typically take about 4–6 weeks after verification.

What Happens After You Submit?

After submission, the Circle Writer reviews the uploaded documents and application details. Where required, the ARO carries out a final check and coordinates or completes the ground inspection.

Once the application is approved and the required conversion fee is paid, the A-Khata is issued digitally. Keep your Application ID safely so you can refer to the application during follow-up.

How Long Does the Conversion Take?

The online process typically takes around 30–60 days when the documents are complete and there are no inspection issues. Offline applications generally take about 4–6 weeks after verification.

Actual processing time can vary depending on document verification, inspection and BBMP processing.

Common Mistakes That Cause Delays

  • Submitting a property tax receipt that does not show nil dues.
  • Pinning the wrong property location on the map.
  • Name mismatch between Aadhaar and the sale deed.
  • Not providing the DC conversion order where it is applicable.

For the detailed fees, documents and wider process, see our B-Khata to A-Khata fees, documents and process guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the first step to convert B-Khata to A-Khata?

Start by gathering the registered sale or title deed, latest property tax receipts, Encumbrance Certificate and other required documents, then apply through the BBMP-GBA e-Aasthi portal.

Can I convert B-Khata to A-Khata online?

Yes. Eligible applicants can use the BBMP-GBA e-Aasthi portal for the online conversion process, including Aadhaar e-KYC, document upload, property-location confirmation and application submission.

What happens after I submit the application?

BBMP verifies the submitted documents and property details and carries out the required inspection. After approval and payment of the applicable conversion fee, the digital A-Khata is issued.

Do I need a lawyer or consultant to convert B-Khata to A-Khata?

No. A lawyer or consultant is not required by law. However, documentation errors can cause delays, so some property owners choose professional assistance with the application and follow-up.
