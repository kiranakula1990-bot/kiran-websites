Who provides Occupancy Certificate consultancy in Bangalore?

Occupancy Certificate consultancy in Bangalore is provided by property and approvals consultants, such as Marvel Consultants, who check the documents, prepare and file the BBMP OC application, answer the authority's queries and follow the file until the certificate is issued. Architects and engineers support the application, because they certify that the completed building matches the sanctioned plan.

In short: the architect certifies the building, and the consultant manages the application, the paperwork and the follow-up.

What is an Occupancy Certificate?

An Occupancy Certificate is issued by the local authority after a building is completed. It generally confirms that the building has been built as per the sanctioned plan and the rules, and that it is fit to be occupied. In Bengaluru, the application goes to the BBMP, which is now part of the Greater Bengaluru Authority and its city corporations.

For the full explanation of the Commencement Certificate and the Occupancy Certificate, read our BBMP Commencement Certificate and Occupancy Certificate guide.

What does an Occupancy Certificate consultant do?

An OC consultant usually covers these parts of the job:

  • Checks the completed building against the sanctioned plan and the approvals.
  • Lists the documents you need and finds what is missing.
  • Works with your architect or engineer on the completion drawings and certificates.
  • Prepares and files the OC application, and pays the authority's fees.
  • Answers queries and remarks, including at the office.
  • Follows the file until the certificate is issued.
  • Supports what comes next: khata, property tax and utility connections.

When do you need an Occupancy Certificate consultant?

You do not always need one. If the building follows the sanctioned plan and your papers are complete, your architect may be able to handle the application. A consultant helps most when:

  • The building differs from the sanctioned plan in some way.
  • Documents are missing, old or do not match each other.
  • The khata or property tax records are not clear.
  • Your application was returned with remarks.
  • You live abroad or cannot spend time at the offices.
  • You are a builder or developer with several units to certify.

Documents commonly needed for an Occupancy Certificate

The list varies by building and by the rules in force. Commonly asked for:

  • Sanctioned building plan and Commencement Certificate
  • Completion certificate and as-built drawings from your architect or engineer
  • Sale deed, khata certificate and extract
  • Latest property tax paid receipt
  • Fire, utility or other NOCs where they apply
  • Owner's identity and address proof
  • Power of attorney, if someone else is applying

The Occupancy Certificate process, step by step

The steps and portals can vary with the rules in force. This is the general flow.

  1. Check the completed building against the sanctioned plan.
  2. Collect the documents listed in Section 5.
  3. Get the completion certificate and drawings from your architect or engineer.
  4. File the OC application online with the fees.
  5. The authority scrutinises the application and may inspect the site.
  6. Answer any remarks quickly and correctly.
  7. The Occupancy Certificate is issued. Check every detail and keep copies.

Why Occupancy Certificate applications get delayed

  • Deviations from the sanctioned plan.
  • Documents that are incomplete or do not match.
  • Pending property tax or khata issues.
  • Missing NOCs.
  • Remarks that are not answered quickly.
  • Applying before the building is actually complete.

Most of these can be found and fixed before you apply. That is the main value of a consultant. Do not trust anyone who promises approval or a fixed number of days.

Why the Occupancy Certificate matters after you finish building

  • Utility connections. Permanent water and power connections generally need it.
  • Khata and property tax. A completed building is generally assessed after the certificate.
  • Bank loans and resale. Buyers and banks usually ask for it.
  • Legal safety. Occupying without it can lead to notices and penalties.

How to choose an Occupancy Certificate consultant

  • Will you check my building against the sanctioned plan before you quote?
  • What is included: documents, filing, follow-up, queries?
  • What are the authority's fees and your fee? Are they shown separately?
  • Who is my contact, and how will I be updated?
  • What happens if the application is returned?

Be careful of anyone who guarantees approval, promises a fixed number of days or asks for payment without a written scope.

Frequently asked questions

What is an Occupancy Certificate (OC) in Bangalore?

An Occupancy Certificate is issued by the local authority after a building is completed. It generally confirms that the building has been built in line with the sanctioned plan and the rules, and is fit to be occupied. In Bengaluru it is applied for through the BBMP, now part of the Greater Bengaluru Authority and its city corporations.

Who provides Occupancy Certificate consultancy in Bangalore?

Occupancy Certificate consultants, such as Marvel Consultants, check the documents, prepare and file the OC application with the BBMP, answer the authority's queries and follow the file until the certificate is issued. Architects and engineers also support the application, because they certify the completed building.

Do I need a consultant to get an Occupancy Certificate?

Not always. If your building follows the sanctioned plan and your papers are complete, your architect may be able to handle it. A consultant helps most when there are deviations, missing documents, a pending khata or tax issue, or when you cannot chase the office yourself.

What documents are needed for an Occupancy Certificate?

Commonly the sanctioned plan and Commencement Certificate, the completion certificate or drawings from your architect or engineer, ownership and khata documents, tax receipts and any required NOCs, such as fire or utility NOCs. The list varies by building, so confirm it before you apply.

What are the BBMP Occupancy Certificate rules?

The authority generally checks that the building was built as per the sanctioned plan, within the permitted limits, with the required approvals in place. The exact rules depend on the building type and the bye-laws in force, which have been changing. Check the current rules for your building before you apply.

How long does an Occupancy Certificate take?

It depends on the completeness of your documents, any deviations found at inspection and the queries raised. Do not rely on a fixed number of days. Complete, matching documents and quick replies are the best way to avoid delay.

What if my building has deviations from the sanctioned plan?

Deviations can delay or block the Occupancy Certificate. What is allowed depends on the extent of the deviation and the rules in force. Get the building checked against the sanctioned plan before you apply, and take advice on the options.

What happens if I occupy a building without an Occupancy Certificate?

You can face problems with utility connections, property tax, bank loans, khata and resale, and the authority may take action. It is better to get the certificate before you occupy the building.
