BBMP B-Khata to A-Khata Conversion in Bangalore: 2026 Complete Guide

What Is B-Khata to A-Khata Conversion?

B-Khata to A-Khata conversion is the process of upgrading a property's BBMP khata from a semi-legal B-Khata to a fully compliant A-Khata, required for a clear title, bank loans, and resale. It runs through the BBMP-GBA e-Aasthi portal. The government's temporary 2% “Bhu Guarantee” discount ended on 23 August 2026 — as of today, the standard conversion fee of 5% of guidance value applies again.

At Marvel Consultants, we've spent over 10 years helping Bengaluru property owners navigate BBMP and GST paperwork without the runaround. This guide covers what B-Khata and A-Khata mean, who qualifies for conversion, what documents you need, current fees, and how the online and offline processes work.

What Changed — The Fee Window Has Closed

The temporary 2% “Bhu Guarantee” conversion fee window ended on 23 August 2026. The standard conversion fee of 5% of guidance value applies from 24 August 2026 onward.

Period Applicable conversion fee
Before 16 May 2026 5% of guidance value
16 May 2026 – 23 August 2026 2% temporary discount
From 24 August 2026 onward 5% standard rate

Before making payment, confirm the exact current figure on the live BBMP-GBA e-Aasthi portal.

What Is the Difference Between A-Khata and B-Khata?

A-Khata represents a property that is recognised as compliant in the BBMP records, while B-Khata generally records properties with certain documentation, approval, or compliance issues. The distinction can affect financing, construction approvals, resale, and access to civic services.

For a broader comparison, see our guide to BDA vs BBMP Khata.

Factor A-Khata B-Khata
Legal recognition Full Partial
Bank loan / mortgage eligibility Yes No
Resale value Higher Lower
Building plan approval Eligible Not eligible
Civic services (water, drainage) Full access Limited

Why Convert to A-Khata

  • Home loans and mortgages become available — most banks will not lend against a B-Khata property.
  • Higher resale value and wider buyer pool.
  • Eligibility for building plan approvals, renovation permits, and completion certificates.
  • Stronger legal standing against future disputes or demolition.
  • Full civic services — BWSSB water, BBMP drainage, and related amenities.
  • Eligibility to apply for BBMP trade licences if a business operates at the property.

Who Can Apply for B-Khata to A-Khata Conversion

Eligible properties

  • Valid B-Khata or final E-Khata with EPID.
  • Property registered on or before 30 September 2024.
  • All property tax dues cleared.
  • Independent house or vacant plot/site, not an apartment flat.
  • Direct public road access.
  • Plots up to 2,000 sq m can be self-applied for; larger plots require a registered architect or engineer.

Not eligible

  • Apartment or multi-unit flat owners.
  • No final E-Khata or EPID.
  • Major unregularised building violations or encroachments.
  • Property registered after 30 September 2024.

If the property is on Panchayat or revenue land, see our guide to Panchayat to BBMP Khata Conversion.

Documents You Will Need

  • Registered sale/title deed.
  • Aadhaar of all owners for OTP e-KYC.
  • Latest property tax receipts showing nil dues.
  • B-Khata/E-Khata with EPID.
  • Latest BESCOM bill.
  • Site plan/building sketch prepared by a licensed architect or engineer.
  • Recent property photographs.
  • DC conversion order, if the property was previously agricultural.
  • Encumbrance Certificate confirming no pending loans or disputes.

For related property-tax information, see our BBMP Property Tax guide.

How the Online Process Works (BBMP-GBA e-Aasthi Portal)

  1. Log in using your mobile number and OTP.
  2. Raise a conversion request and enter the EPID.
  3. Complete Aadhaar e-KYC for every listed owner and receive the Application ID.
  4. Confirm the exact map location of the property.
  5. Declare the revenue survey details and upload the DC order if available.
  6. Upload the required documents and self-certify the application.
  7. Pay the ₹500 application fee and download the acknowledgement.
  8. Complete the physical ground inspection.
  9. After approval, pay the applicable conversion fee and receive the digital A-Khata.

How the Offline Process Works

  1. Check your eligibility.
  2. Visit the jurisdictional ARO/BBMP zonal office and collect the application form.
  3. Submit the completed form, documents, and applicable fees.
  4. BBMP carries out scrutiny and physical verification.
  5. After approval, the conversion is completed, typically within 4–6 weeks.

How Long Does the Conversion Take?

With complete documents and no inspection issues, the online process typically takes 30–60 days. Offline applications generally take about 4–6 weeks after verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is B-Khata to A-Khata conversion?

B-Khata to A-Khata conversion is the process of upgrading a property's BBMP khata from a semi-legal B-Khata to a fully compliant A-Khata, subject to applicable eligibility and approval requirements.

How much does B-Khata to A-Khata conversion cost in Bangalore now?

The standard conversion fee is 5% of the property's guidance value. A separate ₹500 application fee applies, and professional assistance fees, if any, are paid separately.

Is the 2% B-Khata to A-Khata discount still available?

No. The temporary 2% Bhu Guarantee discount window ended on 23 August 2026. The standard 5% conversion fee applies from 24 August 2026 onward.

Where do I apply for B-Khata to A-Khata conversion?

Eligible applicants can apply through the BBMP-GBA e-Aasthi portal. The process involves mobile OTP login, EPID details, Aadhaar e-KYC, document upload, property-location confirmation, inspection, and payment.

What documents are needed?

You generally need the registered sale or title deed, Aadhaar cards of all owners, latest property tax receipts showing nil dues, B-Khata or E-Khata with EPID, latest BESCOM bill, site plan or building sketch, recent property photographs, a DC conversion order if applicable, and an Encumbrance Certificate.

How long does the B-Khata to A-Khata conversion take?

With complete documents and no inspection issues, the online process typically takes 30 to 60 days. Offline applications generally take about 4 to 6 weeks after verification.

Can flat owners apply for this conversion?

No. This conversion process applies to eligible independent houses and vacant plots. Owners of flats in multi-unit apartment buildings are not eligible under this process.
