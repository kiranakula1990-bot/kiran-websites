How to Apply for BBMP E Khata Online in Bangalore — Step-by-Step Guide for Property Owners

Home  ›  Blog  ›  Apply for BBMP E Khata Online

August 25, 2026  |  E-Khata Guide

Owning a property in Bangalore requires proper documentation and updated municipal property records. BBMP E Khata has become an important part of property documentation as property records continue to move toward digital platforms.

With the introduction of BBMP E Aasthi, property owners can access and manage certain property-related information digitally. However, many property owners are still unsure about the BBMP E Khata application process, required documents, Khata status, Khata bifurcation, and the difference between A Khata and B Khata.

This guide explains the BBMP E Khata online application process, documents required, common problems, B Khata to A Khata conversion, and other important information property owners should know.

What is BBMP E Khata?

BBMP E Khata is the digital form of a property's Khata record maintained through the applicable BBMP digital property record system.

It contains important information related to the property, owner, taxation, and property identification. Digital property records can make it easier for owners to access and manage their information.

Property owners commonly search for:

  • BBMP E Khata
  • BBMP E Aasthi
  • BBMP Khata
  • A Khata BBMP
  • B Khata BBMP
  • B Khata to A Khata conversion
  • Khata bifurcation BBMP
  • BBMP Khata transfer

An updated Khata record can be important for property tax, property transactions, documentation, and other municipal processes, depending on the property and applicable requirements.

What is BBMP E Aasthi?

BBMP E Aasthi refers to the digital property record system through which property-related information can be maintained and accessed electronically.

The move toward digital property records is intended to improve accessibility, transparency, and record management.

Depending on the applicable service and property record, property owners may be able to:

  • Access property-related information
  • View available digital property records
  • Check property and tax details
  • Submit applicable property-related applications
  • Request corrections or updates
  • Track applicable application information
  • Access Khata-related records

Property owners should ensure that the information available online matches their registered property documents.

How to Apply for BBMP E Khata Online

The exact process may vary depending on the property type, existing Khata status, and applicable BBMP procedures. However, property owners can generally follow these steps.

Step 1: Check Your Existing Property Records

Before applying for E Khata, first verify the property's existing records.

Check details such as:

  • Property owner's name
  • Property identification number
  • Property address
  • Site or property measurements
  • Property tax details
  • Existing Khata information
  • Previous property documents

Any mismatch should ideally be identified before submitting an application.

Step 2: Keep Your Property Documents Ready

Prepare all applicable documents before beginning the application.

Common documents may include:

  • Registered sale deed
  • Previous title documents
  • Property tax paid receipts
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
  • Existing Khata certificate or extract
  • Owner's identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Property details
  • Building approval documents, where applicable
  • Relevant conversion documents, where applicable

Additional documents may be requested depending on the property and application type.

Step 3: Access the Applicable BBMP Digital Property Service

Property owners can access the applicable BBMP digital property record service and look for the relevant E Khata or property record option.

The available options may vary based on the property record and the services currently provided through the portal.

Step 4: Search or Verify Property Details

Enter the required property information and verify the details displayed in the digital record.

Carefully check:

  • Owner name
  • Property number
  • Address
  • Property dimensions
  • Tax information
  • Khata information
  • Other available property details

If the information is incorrect, the correction process may need to be completed before proceeding.

Step 5: Submit the Required Information

Enter the requested details and upload the required documents in the applicable format.

Make sure that:

  • Documents are clear and readable
  • Names match across documents
  • Property details are consistent
  • Required fields are completed correctly
  • Supporting documents are uploaded properly

Incorrect or incomplete information can result in delays or requests for clarification.

Step 6: Pay Applicable Fees or Charges

Depending on the application and applicable requirements, property owners may need to pay relevant fees or charges.

Always verify the applicable amount through the official process before making a payment.

Step 7: Application Verification

After submission, the application and supporting documents may be reviewed by the concerned authority.

The application may require additional verification if:

  • Property details do not match
  • Documents are incomplete
  • Tax records contain discrepancies
  • Ownership information differs
  • Building or approval information requires clarification

Step 8: Track the Application

After submitting the application, retain the application or acknowledgement details.

Use the applicable digital service to check the status whenever a tracking facility is available.

Step 9: Obtain the Updated Digital Record

Once the application is successfully processed, the updated digital property record can be accessed through the applicable BBMP system, subject to the service and property eligibility.

Documents Required for BBMP E Khata

The exact document requirements can vary. Commonly requested documents may include:

Property owners should verify the current requirements before submitting an application.

A Khata vs B Khata BBMP

Understanding the difference between A Khata and B Khata is important before starting any property-related application.

What is A Khata BBMP?

An A Khata generally relates to a property that meets the applicable municipal requirements and has the necessary approvals and documentation.

Depending on the property and applicable regulations, A Khata properties may have advantages such as:

  • Better documentation status
  • Easier property transactions
  • Better access to certain financial services
  • Eligibility for applicable building permissions
  • Better resale prospects

However, Khata alone should not be considered a complete guarantee of legal title. Property owners should verify the complete property documentation.

What is B Khata BBMP?

A B Khata generally refers to properties recorded separately for taxation purposes where there may be deviations, incomplete approvals, or other compliance-related issues.

Depending on the specific issue, B Khata properties may face:

  • Difficulties with certain building approvals
  • Challenges with some financial institutions
  • Additional documentation requirements
  • Complications during property transactions
  • Need for further regularization or compliance

Each B Khata property should be assessed individually to determine the appropriate course of action.

B Khata to A Khata Conversion

Property owners with B Khata records may want to explore whether their property is eligible for B Khata to A Khata conversion.

Conversion is not automatic and depends on the property's documents, land status, approvals, building compliance, and applicable rules.

General Process for B Khata to A Khata Conversion

The process may generally involve:

1. Property Document Verification

Review the sale deed, title documents, tax records, Khata records, EC, and other relevant documents.

2. Check Compliance

Identify any missing approvals, building deviations, land-related issues, or other compliance requirements.

3. Clear Property Tax Dues

Check for outstanding property tax and other applicable dues and clear them where required.

4. Prepare the Application

Prepare the applicable application and supporting documents according to the current procedure.

5. Pay Applicable Charges

Where applicable, conversion, betterment, regularization, or other charges may need to be paid.

6. Verification

The submitted documents and property details may be reviewed by the concerned authority.

7. Updated Khata Record

If the property meets the applicable requirements and the application is approved, the Khata record may be updated accordingly.

Because eligibility can vary from property to property, professional document verification can help identify potential issues before applying.

Khata Bifurcation BBMP

Khata bifurcation is the process of separating an existing property record into separate records when a property is divided or ownership is separated.

Khata bifurcation may be required when:

  • Property is inherited by multiple legal heirs
  • Joint owners divide their property
  • A larger property is legally divided
  • A site is subdivided
  • Ownership is separated after partition
  • Separate property records are required for eligible units

Documents for Khata Bifurcation

Depending on the circumstances, documents may include:

  • Sale deed
  • Partition deed
  • Gift deed, where applicable
  • Will or inheritance documents, where applicable
  • Property tax receipts
  • Existing Khata documents
  • Identity proof
  • Relevant property plans
  • Other supporting documents

The exact requirements depend on the reason for bifurcation and the property's documentation.

BBMP Khata Transfer

BBMP Khata transfer may be required when ownership of a property changes due to a sale, inheritance, gift, or other legally recognized transfer.

Before applying for Khata transfer, property owners should ensure that:

  • The ownership document is properly registered
  • Property tax records are updated
  • Property details are accurate
  • Supporting documents are available
  • Any applicable dues are cleared

The required procedure may vary depending on the nature of the ownership transfer.

Common Problems During BBMP E Khata Application

Property owners may face several issues during the application process.

Incorrect Owner Details

The owner's name may be different across the sale deed, tax record, Khata record, or digital property record.

Property Number Mismatch

Differences in property numbers or identification details can cause verification issues.

Tax Record Discrepancies

Pending taxes or differences in tax records may need to be resolved.

Missing Documents

Applications may be delayed when required supporting documents are unavailable or incomplete.

Building Approval Issues

Properties with deviations or missing approvals may face additional complications.

Digital Record Errors

Incorrect information in the existing digital record may require a correction request before completing another property-related process.

How to Avoid BBMP E Khata Application Delays

Property owners can reduce avoidable delays by:

  • Checking all property documents before applying
  • Ensuring the owner's name is consistent
  • Verifying property measurements
  • Checking property tax status
  • Keeping the latest EC available
  • Scanning documents clearly
  • Checking the application details before submission
  • Retaining acknowledgement and application details
  • Resolving known discrepancies before applying

Frequently Asked Questions About BBMP E Khata

Can I apply for BBMP E Khata online?

Depending on the property's existing records and the service currently available, property owners may be able to access applicable BBMP digital property services online. The exact process can vary based on the property and application type.

What is BBMP E Aasthi?

BBMP E Aasthi refers to the digital property record system used to maintain and access property-related information electronically.

What documents are required for BBMP E Khata?

Common documents include a registered sale deed, property tax receipts, Encumbrance Certificate, identity proof, existing Khata documents, and other applicable property records.

How can I check my BBMP E Khata status?

If status tracking is available for your application, you can use the applicable BBMP digital property service with your application or acknowledgement details.

Can B Khata be converted to A Khata?

Some properties may be eligible for conversion depending on their documents, land status, approvals, building compliance, and applicable government rules. Eligibility should be checked before applying.

What is Khata bifurcation?

Khata bifurcation is the process of separating a property record into individual records when a property is divided, inherited, partitioned, or otherwise becomes eligible for separate records.

Can I correct wrong information in my E Khata?

If there is incorrect information in the digital property record, a correction may be possible through the applicable procedure. Supporting documents may be required to establish the correct information.

How long does BBMP E Khata processing take?

Processing time can vary depending on the application type, document verification, property records, discrepancies, and the concerned authority's processing procedures.

Is A Khata proof of ownership?

A Khata is an important municipal property record, but it should not be treated as a substitute for a registered title document. Property owners should verify the complete title and supporting legal documents.

Can a consultant help with BBMP E Khata?
