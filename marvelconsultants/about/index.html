The firm

About Marvel Consultants

A Bengaluru firm that knows the offices, reads the records and says plainly what can and cannot be done.

Marvel Consultants was set up in Bengaluru to do the part of property and tax work that most advisers avoid: the government office. For over a decade we have handled BBMP documentation, Khata and e-Khata, property tax, plan approvals and document extraction at sub-registrar, revenue and municipal offices — and represented businesses before the GST department in compliance, disputes and appeals.

Our clients are property owners who need a record corrected, developers who need approvals and unit Khatas, and businesses who need a notice answered. What they have in common is a process that is opaque from the outside and routine to us.

How we work

  • Assess first. A free first look at the documents, and a written note of what applies, what is missing and what it costs — before you commit.
  • Execute end-to-end. We prepare, file, attend, answer queries and follow up. You are not handed a checklist.
  • Plain language. You will know what is being filed and why. No jargon, no guarantees we cannot keep.
  • Fixed fees. Professional fees agreed in advance; government fees paid to the authority, always separate.
  • Confidentiality. Property and tax matters are private. We operate with discretion and do not publish client details without permission.

The team

Sanjay and Kiran lead client work and are the people you speak to — not a helpline. They are supported by a small team handling documentation, filings and office follow-up across Bengaluru's zones. Ask us about relevant experience for your matter; we will discuss it on a call rather than make claims here.

What we are — and are not

We are consultants for property documentation, BBMP/BDA processes and GST representation. We are not a law firm: title opinions, litigation over ownership and court matters need an advocate, and we will say so and refer you. We are not a government agency, and we cannot promise an approval or a timeline that depends on an authority. We are not a documentation marketplace: we take on the cases that need judgement and follow-up, and we tell you when you can do a simple one yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Since when has Marvel Consultants been operating?

The firm has worked on BBMP documentation and GST matters in Bengaluru for over a decade.

Are you affiliated with BBMP or the GST department?

No. We are a private professional services firm. We deal with these authorities on clients' behalf as authorised representatives.

Do you have advocates and chartered accountants on the team?

We work with advocates and CAs on matters that need their certification or opinion, and coordinate so you have one point of contact. Where a matter is primarily legal, we refer you.
