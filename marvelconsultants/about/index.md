A Bengaluru firm that knows the offices, reads the records and says plainly what can and cannot be done.

Marvel Consultants is a Bengaluru-based professional services firm focused on property documentation, legal-service coordination, BBMP/BDA processes and government liaisoning. Since 2014, we have helped property owners, NRIs and real-estate developers navigate records, approvals and documentation that often require coordination across multiple offices.

Our role is practical: understand the objective, review the available records, identify what is missing, prepare the required documentation, coordinate specialist legal support where applicable, file and follow up with the relevant authorities, and keep the client informed until the engagement is completed.

## How we work

- **Assess first.** A free first look at the documents, and a written note of what applies, what is missing and what it costs — before you commit.
- **Execute end-to-end.** We prepare, file, attend, answer queries and follow up. You are not handed a checklist.
- **Plain language.** You will know what is being filed and why. No jargon, no guarantees we cannot keep.
- **Fixed fees.** Professional fees agreed in advance; government fees paid to the authority, always separate.
- **Confidentiality.** Property and tax matters are private. We operate with discretion and do not publish client details without permission.

## What we are — and are not

Marvel Consultants provides property documentation, verification, registration support and government liaisoning. We are **not** a law firm: where a matter requires a formal legal opinion, legal representation or other professional advocacy services, the work is undertaken by enrolled advocates / associated legal counsel as applicable — coordinated within your engagement so you keep one point of contact. We are **not** a government agency, and we cannot promise an approval or a timeline that depends on an authority. We are **not** a documentation marketplace: we take on the cases that need judgement and follow-up, and we tell you when you can do a simple one yourself.
