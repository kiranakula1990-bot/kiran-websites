How to Apply for BBMP E-Khata Online – Step-by-Step Process

Follow these steps to apply online for your BBMP e-Khata via the official e-Aasthi portal:

  1. Visit the official BBMP e-Aasthi portal at bbmpeaasthi.karnataka.gov.in.
  2. Log in with your registered mobile number, or sign up if you are a first-time user.
  3. Search your property using the PID or EPID number issued by BBMP.
  4. Upload required documents — Aadhaar, sale deed, encumbrance certificate, latest tax receipt and property photographs.
  5. Pay the mutation fee online (2% of stamp duty paid at registration, minimum ₹500).
  6. Track your application — this is the simple e-Khata status check tool on the portal using your reference number.
  7. Once verified by the Assistant Revenue Officer (ARO), download your verified e-Khata for ₹125.

B-Khata to A-Khata Conversion — Get Your E-Khata Regularised

A B-Khata is issued to properties with pending approvals, deviations from sanctioned plans, or land that has not been formally regularised. While B-Khata lets you pay property tax, it is not accepted by banks for home loans and is not treated as a title deed during property sales.

Under the Government of Karnataka's November 2025 regularisation drive, B-Khata holders can now apply online via the BBMP e-Aasthi portal to convert their record to a Final A-Khata. This B-Khata to A-Khata conversion in Bengaluru requires clearing outstanding property tax dues, paying applicable Betterment Charges (typically 0.5% to 1.5% of land value), and ensuring proper DC conversion is in place.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BBMP E-Khata?

BBMP E-Khata is the digital version of your Khata (property ownership and tax record) used by Bangalore civic authorities.

Is E-Khata mandatory in Bengaluru?

Yes. BBMP requires e-Khata for building approvals, property registration, and most civic processes.

How long does an E-Khata take?

Time varies based on documentation accuracy and BBMP processing times. Our service accelerates approvals with correct submissions.

Can you transfer E-Khata after buying a property?

Yes — we assist with complete transfer and name update on the e-Khata.

What is a Draft e-Khata?

A Draft e-Khata is an auto-generated digital record of your property based on legacy data. It is a pre-verified document that acts as a starting point for digitization but carries no legal weight for property sales or bank loans.

Why do I need a Final e-Khata?

The Final e-Khata is the mandatory digital certificate for property owners in Bengaluru. It is a secure, QR-coded document required for:

  • Legal Transactions: Buying or selling property.
  • Financial Approvals: Applying for home loans or mortgages.
  • Permissions: Securing building plan sanctions and electricity connections.
How do I upgrade from Draft to Final?

To finalize your record, you must complete the Digital Validation process:

  • e-KYC: Link your Aadhaar and contact details to the property record.
  • Document Upload: Submit your registered sale deed and the latest tax receipts.
  • Verification: Once the Assistant Revenue Officer (ARO) verifies your documents, the status is updated from "Draft" to "Final."
A-Khata vs. B-Khata: The Digital Difference
  • A-Khata: Issued to properties that are fully compliant with all building bylaws and have clear land titles.
  • B-Khata: A digital record for properties with pending approvals or deviations. While it allows you to pay property taxes, it is not a "title" deed.
Can I convert a B-Khata to an A-Khata?

Yes. This is possible by regularizing the property. You must clear all outstanding dues, pay the required "Betterment Charges," and ensure the land has proper DC conversion. Once compliant, you can apply for a migration to the Final A-Khata status.

What is the fee for BBMP E-Khata in 2026?

The mutation fee is 2% of stamp duty paid at registration (minimum ₹500), plus ₹125 for e-Khata download and a small service charge of ₹40–₹45.

How do I check my BBMP E-Khata application status?

You can check your status on the BBMP e-Aasthi portal (bbmpeaasthi.karnataka.gov.in) using your application number or PID. Marvel Consultants also proactively tracks your application status on your behalf.

Can I get E-Khata for a flat or apartment in Bengaluru?

Yes. E-Khata applies to all residential properties within BBMP limits including flats and apartments. The property must have an Occupancy Certificate and comply with BBMP building regulations.
