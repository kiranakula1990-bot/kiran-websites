How we work with businesses

Consultative, representation-led — not a filing factory

Every business engagement starts with a review of where you stand: registrations, return history, ITC position and any open notices. We tell you what is urgent, what is routine and what can wait.

  1. Review

    We read your GST portal, notices and recent returns. Often we find the real issue is a reconciliation gap rather than a tax liability.

  2. Plan

    A written note of what needs filing or replying, by when, and what documents your team must pull together.

  3. Execute & represent

    We prepare filings and replies, attend hearings and liaise with the officer. You approve everything before it goes.

  4. Keep it compliant

    Monthly compliance calendar, reconciliation and alerts so the next notice does not arrive.

Business FAQs

Do you handle GST matters outside Bengaluru?

Yes for Karnataka registrations and the state GST offices; for other states we handle the portal-based work and coordinate local representation where a physical hearing is required.

Can you take over compliance from our existing accountant mid-year?

Yes. We start with a reconciliation of the returns filed so far against the books and GSTR-2B, fix what is fixable in the next return, and document what cannot be changed.

What do you need from us to reply to a notice?

The notice, portal login access or the downloaded documents, the relevant returns, purchase and sales registers for the period, and any prior correspondence. We tell you exactly which items after reading the notice.

Do I need to visit your office to start?

No. Most matters start with a call or WhatsApp and photographs of your documents. We meet at our Yeshwanthpur office or at the property when a physical inspection, signature or original document is needed.

How are your fees structured?

A fixed professional fee agreed before work starts, quoted after a free first assessment of your documents. Government fees, stamp duty and statutory charges are separate and paid to the authority. We do not quote a headline price for complex matters because the work depends on what the records show.

Can you guarantee the approval or the timeline?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Approvals rest with BBMP, BDA, the sub-registrar or the GST department. What we control is a complete, correct application, follow-up with the office, and honest communication about where it stands.

We reply on the same working day. No documents are needed to start.
