GST Registration, Compliance & Filing Services in Bangalore

Why Choose Us?

Navigating the GST regime is complex and time consuming, with disputes often arising from conflicting legal interpretations. Issues such as Input Tax Credit (ITC) allocation, documentation discrepancies, and invoice-return mismatches can lead to non-compliance and litigation. At Marvel Consultants, we specialize in resolving GST disputes, ensuring compliance, and representing clients in legal matters.

Our services include

• Resolving ITC distribution disputes for Input Service Distributors (ISD)
• Addressing allocation methodology disputes
• Ensuring compliance with procedural and documentation requirements
• Representing clients before GST authorities in disputes and appeals

Value that we deliver to our client

As experienced GST consultants, we offer a range of services to help you understand your rights and obligations under the law. We represent our clients in dealing with the GST department, responding to queries from tax officials, and assisting with appeals and disputes. Our expertise simplifies GST litigation, helping you focus on growing your business while we handle the complexities.
