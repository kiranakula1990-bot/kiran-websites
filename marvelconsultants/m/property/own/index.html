Owning property in Bengaluru: keep the record right

Last reviewed 2026-08-25 · Bengaluru

Ownership in Bengaluru has two layers: the registered deed, and BBMP's record of the property — Khata, e-Khata and the property-tax account. The deed rarely changes; the record must be kept current every time something happens: you buy, a parent passes away, you build, you extend, you rent to a business, or the city redraws a ward.

This journey covers the routine and not-so-routine tasks of keeping the record right, so that when you need a loan, a sanction or a sale, nothing is waiting to be fixed.

Does this sound like you?

You bought recently and the Khata is still in the seller's name

File the Khata transfer now; tax notices and the official record follow the Khata, not the deed.

You have a paper Khata but nothing on e-Aasthi

Create the e-Khata — it is needed for registration, and increasingly for other BBMP services.

Your e-Khata shows the wrong name, spelling, dimensions or property number

Corrections go through BBMP with supporting documents; better done now than at sale time.

You built, extended or changed the use of the property

The property-tax assessment must be revised; under-declared area is a common reason for later demands with penalty.

You received a property-tax demand that looks wrong

Assessment corrections and appeals exist — but they are time-bound.

The property came to you by inheritance or partition

Khata transfer with legal-heir documents, and bifurcation if the property is being divided among heirs.

What usually needs to be done

  1. Record check

    We pull the current Khata, e-Khata and tax details from BBMP's systems and compare them with your deed and tax receipts.

  2. Corrections and transfers

    Khata transfer, e-Khata creation or correction, name/area/usage corrections, bifurcation or amalgamation — each with the ward-office paperwork it needs.

  3. Property tax

    Self-assessment for new or changed property, correction of past years, arrears settlement and appeals.

  4. Keep it current

    A one-page record of what is on file, plus reminders for tax due dates.

Documents to gather

  • Registered sale deed / gift deed / partition deed / will and legal-heir certificate
  • Existing Khata certificate and extract, if any
  • Property-tax-paid receipts
  • Encumbrance Certificate (latest)
  • Sanctioned plan / occupancy certificate for buildings
  • Identity and address proof of all owners

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a Khata certificate and a Khata extract?

The certificate confirms that a Khata exists in a named owner's name for a property; the extract gives the details recorded — property number, dimensions, built-up area, use and tax assessment. Banks and buyers usually want both.

How do I check if my property has an e-Khata?

Search on BBMP's e-Aasthi portal using the property identification details or owner name; the guide How to apply for BBMP e-Khata online walks through it. If nothing appears, or the details are wrong, the e-Khata needs to be created or corrected.

I pay property tax every year. Does that mean my Khata is fine?

Not necessarily. Tax can be paid on a B Khata property, or in a previous owner's name. Tax receipts prove payment, not ownership or regularity — check the Khata and e-Khata separately.
