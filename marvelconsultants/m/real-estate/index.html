Developer FAQs

Do you replace our architect or advocate?

No. The architect prepares drawings and the advocate gives the title opinion. We handle the government-facing process — eligibility, documentation, filing, liaison and follow-up — and coordinate with both.

Can you get Khata for a project where the parent Khata is B Khata?

Sometimes. It depends on the layout's approval status, conversion and compliance. We assess eligibility for A Khata conversion first; if it is not eligible, we tell you plainly and explain what the B Khata means for buyers.

How do you charge for project work?

A scoped fee per stage or per unit (for Khata/e-Khata work), agreed in writing. Government fees, betterment charges and statutory levies are separate and paid to the authority.

Can you guarantee the approval or the timeline?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Approvals rest with BBMP, BDA, the Sub-Registrar or the concerned authority. What we control is a complete, correct application, follow-up with the office, and honest communication about where it stands.

We reply on the same working day. No documents are needed to start.

Planning a project, or stuck on one?

Tell us the site and the stage. We will say what is needed and what is realistic.
