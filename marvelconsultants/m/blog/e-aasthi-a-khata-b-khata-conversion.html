BBMP E Khata: Complete Guide to E Aasthi, A Khata, B Khata & Conversion

Home  ›  Blog  ›  BBMP E Khata Complete Guide

August 25, 2026  |  E-Khata Guide

Property documentation is an important part of owning, selling, transferring, or developing a property in Bangalore. With the digitization of property records, BBMP E Khata and BBMP E Aasthi have become important terms for property owners.

The Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) has introduced digital systems to make property records easier to access and manage. A Khata record contains important information related to property ownership, identification, taxation, and other property details.

Property owners commonly search for information about BBMP E Khata, BBMP E Aasthi, A Khata, B Khata, B Khata to A Khata conversion, and Khata bifurcation when they need to update or regularize their property records.

This guide explains the basics of BBMP E Khata, the difference between A Khata and B Khata, the conversion process, required documents, Khata bifurcation, and common issues faced by property owners.

What is BBMP E Aasthi (BBMP E Khata)?

BBMP E Aasthi refers to the digital property record system through which property-related information can be maintained electronically. BBMP E Khata provides a digital representation of property records that were traditionally maintained through physical Khata documents.

The digital record can contain information such as:

  • Property identification details
  • Owner information
  • Property tax information
  • Property measurements and details
  • Khata-related information
  • Other applicable property records

The digitization of property records can make it easier for property owners to access and manage their information.

Benefits of BBMP E Khata

Some of the key benefits of maintaining updated digital property records include:

  • Easier access to property information
  • Convenient property tax-related processes
  • Better record management
  • Reduced dependency on physical documents
  • Easier verification of property information
  • Support for property-related transactions and applications, subject to applicable rules

Property owners should ensure that the information recorded in their digital property records is accurate and matches their supporting documents.

Understanding A Khata and B Khata

One of the most common questions among Bangalore property owners is the difference between A Khata and B Khata.

What is A Khata?

An A Khata generally refers to a property record associated with properties that meet the applicable municipal requirements and have the necessary approvals and documentation.

Depending on the property and applicable regulations, A Khata properties may have advantages such as:

  • Better eligibility for property-related transactions
  • Easier access to certain financial services
  • Better resale prospects
  • Ability to apply for applicable building permissions
  • Improved documentation status

However, having an A Khata does not by itself replace the need to verify title, approvals, land-use permissions, and other legal requirements.

What is B Khata?

A B Khata generally refers to properties recorded separately for taxation purposes where there may be deviations, incomplete approvals, or other issues affecting the property's compliance status.

A B Khata property may face limitations depending on the nature of the issue, including:

  • Difficulty obtaining certain approvals
  • Challenges with some financial institutions
  • Complications during property transactions
  • Restrictions related to building permissions
  • Difficulty regularizing or updating property records

Property owners should have their documents reviewed to understand the specific status of their property rather than relying only on the Khata category.

BBMP B Khata to A Khata Conversion

Many property owners want to convert their B Khata to A Khata to improve their property's documentation and compliance status.

However, conversion is not automatic. The property's eligibility depends on several factors, including the property documents, land status, approvals, building compliance, and applicable government regulations.

General Steps for B Khata to A Khata Conversion

The process may generally involve the following steps:

1. Verify Property Documents

The first step is to review the available property documents and identify any discrepancies, missing documents, or compliance issues.

Documents may include:

  • Sale deed
  • Previous title documents
  • Property tax receipts
  • Encumbrance Certificate
  • Khata documents
  • Building plan or approval documents
  • Identity and address proof
  • Other applicable property documents

2. Check Property and Building Compliance

The property should be examined for applicable land, building, planning, and municipal requirements.

Any deviation or missing approval should be identified before proceeding.

3. Clear Outstanding Property Taxes

Pending property taxes and other applicable dues should be checked and cleared as required.

4. Submit the Application

Once the property documents and eligibility are verified, the applicable application for Khata conversion can be submitted along with the required supporting documents.

5. Pay Applicable Charges

Depending on the property and applicable rules, the owner may need to pay applicable conversion, betterment, regularization, or other charges.

6. Verification and Processing

The submitted documents and property details may be reviewed by the concerned authority.

If discrepancies are identified, additional documents or corrections may be required.

7. Updated Khata Record

After successful processing and approval, the property's Khata record may be updated according to the applicable process.

Because requirements can vary depending on the property, professional documentation assistance can help identify potential issues before submitting an application.

Khata Bifurcation BBMP — When Is It Required?

Khata bifurcation refers to separating a property record into individual records when a property is divided or ownership is separated.

Khata bifurcation may be required in situations such as:

  • Property inheritance
  • Division among family members
  • Joint property separation
  • Plot subdivision
  • Property partition
  • Certain apartment or unit-related situations

After successful bifurcation, separate property records may be maintained for the respective properties or owners, subject to applicable rules and approvals.

Why is Khata Bifurcation Important?

Khata bifurcation can help ensure that:

  • Each property has appropriate individual records
  • Property tax responsibilities are separated
  • Ownership-related records are properly maintained
  • Future property transactions are easier to document
  • Individual property details can be maintained separately

The exact requirements depend on the type of property and reason for bifurcation.

Why is BBMP Khata Important?

A properly maintained Khata record can be important for several property-related activities.

Property owners may need updated Khata documentation for processes such as:

  • Property tax payment
  • Property sale or purchase
  • Property transfer
  • Bank loan applications
  • Building-related applications
  • Property documentation
  • Certain registration and municipal processes

However, Khata should not be considered the only document required to establish ownership or legal title. Property owners should also verify their sale deed, title documents, approvals, EC, and other applicable records.

Documents Required for BBMP E Khata

The documents required may vary depending on the property and the type of application.

Common documents may include:

  • Registered sale deed
  • Previous title documents
  • Property tax paid receipts
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
  • Owner's identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Existing Khata certificate or extract, where applicable
  • Property identification details
  • Building plan or approval documents, where applicable
  • Relevant conversion or sanction documents
  • Other supporting documents requested by the concerned authority

For B Khata to A Khata conversion, additional documents may be required depending on the property's status and the reason for conversion.

Before applying, property owners should verify that the names, measurements, survey details, property numbers, and other information are consistent across the documents.

Common Problems During BBMP E Khata Applications

Property owners may face several issues while applying for or updating digital Khata records.

Common problems include:

Incorrect Property Details

The owner's name, property number, measurements, address, or other details may not match the supporting documents.

Missing Documents

Applications can be delayed when required documents are unavailable or incomplete.

Property Tax Issues

Outstanding taxes or discrepancies in tax records may need to be resolved before certain processes can proceed.

Khata Bifurcation Issues

Bifurcation applications may require additional documentation when ownership or property boundaries are unclear.

B Khata to A Khata Conversion Issues

Conversion may become complicated when there are building deviations, missing approvals, land-related issues, or other compliance concerns.

Digital Record Errors

Errors in existing digital property records may need to be corrected through the applicable procedure.

Professional document verification before submitting an application can help reduce avoidable delays and errors.

Frequently Asked Questions About BBMP E Khata

What is BBMP E Khata?

BBMP E Khata is the digital version of a property's Khata record maintained through the applicable BBMP digital property record system. It contains important property and tax-related information.

What is BBMP E Aasthi?

BBMP E Aasthi refers to the digital property record system used for maintaining and accessing property-related information electronically.

What is the difference between A Khata and B Khata?

A Khata generally relates to properties that meet applicable requirements and have the necessary approvals and documentation. B Khata may relate to properties with certain deviations, incomplete approvals, or other compliance issues.

Can B Khata be converted to A Khata?

B Khata to A Khata conversion may be possible for eligible properties depending on the property's documents, land status, approvals, building compliance, and applicable government rules. Each property needs to be assessed individually.

What documents are required for BBMP E Khata?

Common documents include the sale deed, property tax receipts, Encumbrance Certificate, identity proof, property details, and applicable building approval documents. Additional documents may be required depending on the application.

What is Khata bifurcation?

Khata bifurcation is the process of separating a property record into individual records when a property is divided, inherited, partitioned, or otherwise becomes eligible for separate records.

Why is BBMP E Khata important?

BBMP E Khata helps property owners maintain and access digital property records. Updated records can be useful for property tax, documentation, transactions, and other property-related processes.

Can mistakes in BBMP E Khata be corrected?

Property owners may be able to request corrections to errors in their digital property records through the applicable procedure. The documents required depend on the nature of the correction.

How long does BBMP E Khata processing take?

Processing time can vary depending on the application type, document verification, property records, corrections required, and the concerned authority's processing procedures.

Is Khata the same as property ownership proof?

No. Khata is an important municipal property record, but it should not be treated as a substitute for a registered title document. Property owners should verify the complete chain of title and other applicable legal documents.

Can professional consultants help with B Khata to A Khata conversion?

Yes. Professional consultants can help review property documents, identify potential documentation issues, prepare applicable paperwork, and provide assistance throughout the relevant process.
